Car Price Prediction API
Production-ready ML API that suggests a resale price for used cars (US market) from make, mileage, horsepower, and body style. EDA-driven preprocessing (imputation, one-hot encoding, filtering out zero-mileage/list-price outliers) feeds a tuned RandomForestRegressor (R² = 0.865, MAE ≈ $2,905, MAPE ≈ 12%). Built with Clean Architecture / SOLID and dependency injection — the domain layer has zero knowledge of sklearn or FastAPI — exposed via a POST /predict endpoint, fully containerized with Docker (docker-compose up --build), and covered by automated pytest tests (3/3 passing).

9 ML Algorithms, from Linear Regression to an LLM
A 70-second visual tour showing that most of machine learning is one idea, a line, bent in smarter ways:
  • Linear & logistic regression: fit a line, then bend it into an S to answer yes / no.
  • Decision trees, random forests & gradient boosting: ask questions, let many trees vote, fix mistakes one tree at a time.
  • k-nearest neighbours & k-means: classify by your neighbours, find groups with no labels.
  • Neural networks & LLMs: stack bent lines into layers, and scale them to billions of numbers that predict the next word.
The examples use real data: house sales, Titanic passengers, iris flowers, and GPT-2 (124,439,808 numbers). Subtitles available in English and Greek.
Source: video and content by Data Science Brain (@datasciencebrain), originally posted on LinkedIn. Shared here for educational purposes; Greek subtitles by Thomas Kopalidis.
